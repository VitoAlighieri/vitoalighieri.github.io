## Detailed findings

### P2

**[P2] Raised text cuts off phone content**
- **Location:** `assets/css/mobile.css:372-379` (the bench `.stack-grid` is `1fr 1fr`, so a column can't shrink below its longest word), `mobile.css:291-296` (the pager's `repeat(3,1fr)` with words that can't break), and the large-text tier at `mobile.css:459-469`, which covers neither. On desktop, `styles.css:698-703` (the `.ch-side` column).
- **Impact:** The text size was raised with `Page.setFontSizes`.
  - **393 px, 150% text, ES:** the third pager button runs to x=452. In the right bench column, "SignalR" and "OBSERVABILIDAD" are cut off (`ts-393-24-es-4.png`).
  - **393 px, 200% text, EN:** "FRAMEWORKS", "ASP.NET" and "Windows Server" are cut off (`ts-393-32-en-4.png`), and so is the pager.
  - **1440 px, 200% text:** the channel traces and their captions run 81–97 px past the viewport.
  - The content isn't hidden by a scrollbar: it is removed by `html{overflow-x:clip}`.
  - This breaks DESIGN.md's Large-Text Rule and the owner's requirement in PRODUCT.md that controls stay stable under text zoom. It relates to WCAG 1.4.4, though browser zoom itself passes: 720 and 360 px viewports show no overflow.
- **Recommendation:** In the large-text tier:
  - Bench: one column (`grid-template-columns:1fr`) and `overflow-wrap:anywhere` on the h3 and li.
  - Pager: make it `repeat(3,minmax(0,1fr))` and let the label wrap under the number.
  - Desktop: give `.ch-side` `min-width:0`.
- **Command:** /impeccable adapt

**[P2] The Spanish pager outgrows its card at 350 px and narrower**
- **Location:** `assets/css/mobile.css:291-297`
- **Impact:** At 320 px (ES), the buttons measure 87, 113 and 96 px against a 264 px pager. "03 LIDERAR" runs to x=323, 3 px off the screen. At 340 px it ends 11 px past the card edge. The amber needle is a third of the pager width, so it no longer sits under the selected button (`pager-320-es.png`). This affects the Galaxy Fold cover screen (344 px) and 320 px phones.
- **Recommendation:** Use `minmax(0,1fr)` tracks plus a compact rule at 380 px and below: shorter tracking, or the number alone with the verb on a second line.
- **Command:** /impeccable adapt

**[P2] The trace makes the hero role line hard to read**
- **Location:** `assets/css/styles.css:605` (the `text-shadow` halo), `app.js:332` (the trace amplitude). The role line is the identity line from PRODUCT.md.
- **Impact:** I hid the text, screenshotted, and compared each text colour with the real pixels behind it. "p05" below is the contrast at the 5th-percentile (worst) pixel behind the text.

  | Condition | Text | p05 | Lowest pixel | Median |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Running, worst of 8 frames | "Cybersecurity", "communication", "project leadership" | 2.3–2.9 | ≈1.0 | 9.4–11 |
  | Frozen frame (HOLD or reduced motion), 1440×900 | "PROJECT LEADERSHIP" | 2.48 | 1.41 | — |
  | Frozen frame, phone | "Janer" | 2.2 (needs 3) | — | — |

  In the frozen frame the trace sits on "PROJECT LEADERSHIP" permanently. That frame is exactly what reduced-motion users get (`d-hero-hold.png`).
- **WCAG:** 1.4.3
- **Recommendation:** Any of these would work:
  - Fade the trace in a band behind `.role` (a mask on the canvas, or lower `uOpacity` in that y-range).
  - Choose a still-frame phase whose crest clears the role line.
  - Give the role line a solid ink pill.
- **Command:** /impeccable harden

**[P2] Systems card numbers say 04.x under the CH·05 code**
- **Location:** `assets/css/mobile.css:355-360` (`content:"04." counter(sys)`)
- **Impact:** On phones the Systems cards read 04.1–04.3, while the section rule, the strip and the dock all read CH·05 (`land-667-es-docked.png`, `forced-summary.png`). This breaks the "one numbering system" rule. It is left over from How I work taking CH·04.
- **Recommendation:** Use `"05."`, or generate the prefix from the section's `data-band`.
- **Command:** /impeccable polish

**[P2] The scope's JS colours bypass the tokens**
- **Location:** `assets/js/app.js:146` (0x223047, off-palette), `182`, `208` (0xFF9A1F, off-palette), `219` (0xFFD9A0), `225`, `235-237`, `394` ('255,201,119', which is not `--amber-2` 255,198,119), `413`, `421`, `776`, `782`; `assets/js/mobile.js:301`
- **Impact:** 21 hard-coded colours. Changing the accent is no longer "one edit", contrary to DESIGN.md's One Triplet Rule, and there are three extra oranges plus a blue-grey graticule that aren't in the palette.
- **Recommendation:** At boot, read `--amber-rgb`, `--amber-2` and `--line` from `getComputedStyle(document.documentElement)` and feed them to THREE.Color and the canvas strokes. Document the halo and core tints as derived steps.
- **Command:** /impeccable colorize

**[P2] The swipe cue animates off-screen on phones until the deck is swiped**
- **Location:** `assets/css/mobile.css:302` (`.ch-swipe::after`, infinite). It only stops through `#channels.deck-seen` (`mobile.css:251`) or HOLD.
- **Impact:** Measured on a phone at 4× CPU, scrolled to Stack, with the strip docked and running:

  | State | Main-thread task (ms/s) | Style recalc (ms/s) |
  |---|---|---|
  | As shipped | 358 | 39 |
  | Swipe cue switched off | 255 | 0 |

  That is about 29% of the docked idle cost. It applies to every phone visitor who never swipes the deck.
- **Recommendation:** Run it only while the cue is in view (an IntersectionObserver toggling a class), or limit it to a few iterations.
- **Command:** /impeccable optimize

**[P2] three.js still loads 670 KB, and startup is heavy on mid-range phones**
- **Location:** `index.html:530`, `assets/vendor/three.min.js`
- **Impact:**
  - three.js is 670 KB raw (166 KB gzipped), 75% of all JS, used for a ribbon, a line, a grid and a sprite.
  - It logs the "build/three.min.js … deprecated" warning on every load.
  - Phone at 4× CPU: long tasks total 2,119 ms in the first 3 s (604, 351 and 338 ms). In the trace, app.js evaluation is 392 ms, including WebGL setup and ScrollTrigger refreshes; UpdateLayoutTree is 659 ms and Layout 597 ms.
  - These numbers are inflated by SwiftShader.
- **Recommendation:** Write a raw WebGL or Canvas2D renderer (the shaders are already hand-written), or use a tree-shaken ES-module build. Batch the startup ScrollTrigger refreshes.
- **Command:** /impeccable optimize

## Positive findings

- **Reduced motion:** the page starts held (`aria-pressed=true`, "Signal — Paused"), the hero and the deck are pixel-identical 1.5 s apart, no CSS animations run, and un-holding works and is remembered.
- **Keyboard:**
  - The undocked strip is `inert`, so Tab starts at the hero.
  - Focus never lands behind the strip or the dock (scroll padding at `mobile.css:93`).
  - Every focus ring is a 2 px amber outline.
  - The channel index opens on the current row, traps Tab, closes on Esc and returns focus to the dock. `main` is `inert` while it is open, and the Android back gesture closes it. Picking a row moves focus to that section's h2.
- **Semantics:** h1 → h2 → h3 → h4 with no skips, and the dial is `aria-hidden`. The copy key reads "Copy the email address", with a polite live region outside the dock. The scrollable deck is a named, focusable region.
- **Contrast:** every static pair passes AA on both layers and in the index; the weakest is `--mute` at 5.0–5.5. The outlined channel numbers are 4.3–4.5:1, and the amber focus ring is 10.6:1.
- **Responsive:**
  - No overflow at 320, 360, 390, 414, 600, 720, 768, 1024 and 1440 px, in EN or ES.
  - Touch targets are ≥44 px apart from the HOLD dot.
  - Gestures:
    - The deck snaps one card per swipe and retunes the dock and strip.
    - A vertical drag on the deck or the hero scrolls the page.
    - A cancelled swipe snaps back.
    - A sideways drag on the hero stretches the trace and springs back; `touchCancel` clears it.
    - Tapping the dock opens the index, and tapping a row navigates.
  - Landscape phones get a compact 48 px dock.
- **Performance:**
  - On slow 4G (uncompressed) at 4× CPU, the hero copy shows at 2.51 s, right at first paint (2.57 s): the 1 s fallback in the head works.
  - The 3D render stops once docked, the strip runs at 30 fps, and the mini traces are gated by visibility.
  - A hidden tab costs about 0 ms/s.
  - There is no backdrop blur on touch devices, and `powerPreference` is `'default'`.
- **Theming:** `color-scheme` is set in both the meta tag and `:root`; selection, scrollbar and focus are themed; forced colours give real Canvas grounds, a ButtonText × and a +/− (verified with emulation).
- **Content:** all 123 `data-i18n` keys have Spanish entries. Without JS, nothing is hidden. No phone number appears, and the venture is not named in either language or in the meta tags.
