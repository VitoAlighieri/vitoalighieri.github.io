## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment.** Authored for this person, but not yet for this buyer. You could not swap the page onto another candidate:
- the scope retunes for each section;
- each channel has its own wave (square = BUILD, noisy triangle = BREAK, step = LEAD);

## Overall Impression

The craft is real. The trace, the strip, the dock, the index and the deck are an engineered instrument, not a shrunken template. The biggest opportunity is in what it says rather than how it looks: the first screen sells "software engineer · 03 yrs" to a visitor who comes to judge whether they can commission an offensive-security engagement.

## What's Working

1. **The instrument concept carries information.** The trace, the strip readout, the dock needle and each card's scope change with your position. The wave shapes encode the character of each channel.
2. **A disciplined type system with a single accent.** Anybody, Archivo and JetBrains Mono, with amber spent only on signal, state and the primary action. The main copy contrast holds: bone-soft 11.98:1, amber 10.76:1.
3. **The phone layer is engineered, not shrunk.** It has a thumb-zone dock, an index with real focus management, a pager synced to the deck, COPY with confirmation in the dock, and Spanish that is adapted rather than translated.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] The page does not lead with what the primary buyer is looking for**
- **What:**
  - The hero shows the name, then "SOFTWARE ENGINEER" in amber with "pentester" in grey.
  - The lede opens with "I build systems", and the only figure is "03 yrs".
  - Screen 2 is the "frequencies" thesis.
  - The channels go BUILD before BREAK, and the phone deck opens on BUILD.
  - The first explicit security scope arrives about 3 screens down on desktop and 4 on phones.
- **Why it matters:** PRODUCT.md says the primary visitor decides "quickly whether Biel is credible and right for their scope". To a CISO, "software engineer + 3 years" reads junior.
- **Fix:**
  - Pentester / offensive security as the amber lead of the roles.
  - A lede that opens with the offer: web, APIs, RF, hardware and ICS/OT, findings mapped to ISO 27001, NIS2 and DORA, keeping "break them on purpose".
  - Swap EXP for a scope readout (WEB · NET · RF · HW · ICS/OT).
  - Order BREAK → BUILD → LEAD, or open the phone deck on 02.
  - Move the thesis after Systems, or cut it to one line.
- **Command:** /impeccable clarify (then /impeccable layout for the reorder).

**[P1] There is no "what working with me looks like" section**
- **What:** The differentiator ("scope, test, explain and tie it to the business") only appears as bullet 08 of 9 inside a card (index.html:273). Nothing describes the process or what the client receives.
- **Why it matters:** With no logos or published case studies (and none may be invented), the process is the most honest credibility available.
- **Fix:**
  - A four-step strip after Systems: Scope → Test → Report (CVSS, mapped to ISO 27001/NIS2/DORA/GDPR) → Retest and hardening.
  - Terms, timing and NDA stay as open owner decisions.
- **Command:** /impeccable shape.

**[P1] The contact moment does not reassure and dilutes the buyer**

- **Command:** /impeccable clarify + /impeccable harden.
