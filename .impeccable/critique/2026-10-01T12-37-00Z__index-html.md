- **What:**
  - The "Certifications" heading holds a Black Hat *training* (OHH), an ESADE *course*, and "CCNA content completed", which the page itself admits is not a certification.

  - eJPT's issuer (INE) is still not named in the list.
- **Why it matters:**
  - PRODUCT.md: "Nothing is claimed that cannot be backed" and "Credibility a buyer can verify".
  - A CISO knows that OSCC-SEC, CJCA, eJPT and PCEP are foundational certifications. Inflated wording around them turns honest entry-level credentials into a credibility deduction.
- **Fix:**
  - Rename the heading to "Certifications & training", or split it into two lists:

    - Training: Black Hat OHH, CCNA coursework, ESADE.
  - Change the summary to "Certified by OffSec, Hack The Box and INE; trained at Black Hat".
  - Drop "red team" unless the owner can back it.
